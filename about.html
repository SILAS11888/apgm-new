About Our Church

The Apostolic Power and Glory Ministry is a Bible-believing church committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ with power, signs, and wonders.

Our mission is to bring salvation, healing, deliverance, restoration, and spiritual growth to every believer through the Word of God and the power of the Holy Spirit.

We believe in holiness, prayer, worship, evangelism, and raising disciples who will impact their generation for Christ.
